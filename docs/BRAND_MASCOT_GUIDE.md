# CardStorm Mascot Brand Guide

This documents the approved CardStorm mascot direction. Read this before generating or
commissioning any new CardStorm mascot artwork - it exists so the mascot stays visually
consistent across hero images, sections, and future scenes instead of drifting art request
to art request.

## Canonical reference image

`assets/mascot/ask-cardstorm-mascot-hero.webp` (also wired in as the hero image on the
Know Thy Worth / Ask CardStorm page in `app.html`) is the **canonical visual reference**
for the mascot going forward. When in doubt about proportions, palette, expression, or prop
design, match this image over any written description here.

## Mascot appearance

- A **card-headed character**: his head/face is a glowing CardStorm playing card, not a
  human face or a generic cartoon head.
- Broad-shouldered, confident, heroic stance - reads as an expert, not a sidekick.
- A pointed hat and a flowing cape are **okay to keep** - they give him a faint
  all-knowing-guide quality without being a literal wizard costume.

## Face / expression direction

- Confident, knowledgeable, a little sly-smart - like he already knows the answer to your
  question before you finish asking it.
- **Do not** make him childish, goofy, or cutesy.
- **Do not** make him threatening, menacing, or villain-coded. He's an expert guide, not an
  intimidating figure.

## Clothing

- Dark navy / black base costume (hat, cape, body armor/plating).
- Gold trim and banding on the armor, hat brim, and cape edge.
- Electric blue glow/lightning worked into the card-face and costume seams - this is his
  "storm energy," not just a lighting effect.

## Color palette

| Role | Color |
|---|---|
| Base / body | Dark navy / near-black |
| Energy / glow | Electric blue |
| Trim / accents | Gold |
| Logo lightning bolt | CardStorm blue-to-gold gradient (matches the official logo - see below) |

## Lightning treatment

- Lightning is CardStorm's signature energy motif: it should feel like it's radiating from
  the character (card-face glow, seams, prop) and crackling in the background/sky of the
  scene - not just a decorative sticker.
- Keep it electric blue. Gold is for trim/metal, not for the lightning itself.

## Baseball bat (primary prop)

This replaces the old generic wizard staff with a crystal/orb. **Do not use a staff or
crystal orb again.**

- Premium dark navy / black bat body.
- Electric blue storm/lightning energy running through or emanating from the bat.
- Gold trim/details (grip band, end cap, accent rings).
- Subtle CardStorm lightning-bolt branding and the "CARDSTORM" wordmark on the barrel.
- Reads clearly as a baseball bat first - unique to CardStorm, but never fantasy weapon-like.
- Approved poses: held over the shoulder, used like a pointer, or planted beside him. It is
  a mascot prop / expert pointer, not a weapon - never pose it in a striking or threatening
  position.

## Golf driver (optional future variation)

For future scenes needing a different sport personality, a matching CardStorm golf driver
may be used instead of the bat:

- Dark navy / black clubhead and shaft.
- Blue electric accents.
- Gold trim.
- Lightning-bolt / CardStorm branding on the clubhead.

Same materials and treatment as the bat - just a different sport, so the mascot doesn't feel
like the same static pose every time he shows up.

## Approved pose style

- Confident and grounded - standing on solid footing (a rock, a stage, a stadium overlook),
  not floating or in an exaggerated action pose.
- Prop can be over the shoulder, used as a pointer, or planted beside him.
- Scene elements (lightning sky, stadium banners, card art) can change per placement, but
  the mascot himself should stay recognizable as the same character from image to image.

## What NOT to use

- ❌ No generic wizard staff.
- ❌ No crystal or orb of any kind, on a staff or otherwise.
- ❌ Nothing that reads as a literal weapon or combat pose.
- ❌ No childish/goofy cartoon treatment.
- ❌ No menacing/villain treatment.
- ❌ Never alter, redraw, or restyle the official CardStorm logo (the card-stack + lightning
  bolt wordmark) - it appears as-is in scenes, it is not a mascot design element to reinterpret.

## Variation guidance

Future hero and section images should reuse this same mascot identity - same head, face
treatment, costume, and palette - but are encouraged to vary the **pose and sports prop**
(bat, golf driver, or a new sport-appropriate prop that follows the same navy/black + electric
blue + gold + lightning-bolt treatment) so the artwork doesn't feel repetitive across the site.
