## Pokémon / TCG

No official Pokémon artwork, box art, or product photography is used
anywhere in CardStorm's Pokémon/TCG section. Per Pokémon Support's own
guidance (https://support.pokemon.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000634094),
the official Pokémon site is used only as a factual data source (set
names, release dates, official database card numbers/rarities) — never
as an image library. All Pokémon/TCG visuals in this app are original
CardStorm-drawn glyphs and color treatments.
